## Concrete implementation decisions

- Keep InternalNote separate from OperationalThread and OperationalMessageDraft. Notes retain SubjectId, bounded body, author label and inherited creator/time, with update/delete SQL guards. Thread retains SubjectId, visibility, optional exact RelationshipId and subject text. Thread audience/parent cannot be edited after creation.
- Draft retains ThreadId, body, author label, state, updated time and rowversion. Normalized recipient and attachment association rows retain exact ContactId and DocumentVersionId, with unique composites and no cascading business-history deletion. A later queue operation snapshots these into immutable MessageVersion; it must not deliver by reading mutable draft rows.
- Extend the existing capability allowlist for internal-note-read/write and message-read/write. Internal agency-admin may use its authorized agency/relationship records; it gains no underwriting parent scope. Finance remains denied until a scoped finance implementation. Actual broker authentication remains out of scope; exercise an explicit agency-safe projection service under trusted agency/relationship context, not a caller-controlled visibility switch that bypasses internal access.
- List cursors bind current actor/scope, subject or thread, page size and filters. Notes and threads have separate counts and queries. Message body DTOs explicitly allow only authored body, recipient labels/IDs, attachment version metadata and provenance; they never serialize support flags or underwriting objects.
- Require current owned active relationship before thread creation and every later operation, including replay. For agency-scoped parents, validate a selected relationship belongs to that agency. For other parents, require their exact relationship. Internal threads reject relationship IDs and external recipient IDs.
- Draft writes allow blank body and empty recipient/attachment selections. Validate no null body, maximum8000 characters, no empty/duplicate IDs, at most50 recipients and20 attachments. Creating a thread requires nonblank subject<=300. Send requirements remain09-10.
- Agency attachment requires exact same subject plus visibility=agency and the thread relationship. Internal thread may reference currently readable same-parent evidence, but never broadens its visibility. Validate current readiness and origin on writes; on reads reauthorize all links and avoid publishing foreign metadata. Recipient rows must be current, same relationship and have valid single addresses; read choices omit ended/invalid contacts.
- Existing document/task confirmation helpers are precedents for fixed operation identity and retained uncertain input. A draft save reports Saved draft only. Source send-to-agency actions open an agency composer with explicitly selected versions; no send receipt until09-10.
