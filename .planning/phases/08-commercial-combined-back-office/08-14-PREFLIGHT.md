## Exposure design detail to resolve before14 implementation

CommercialExposureProjection.AppendAsync already creates empty locations for a cancellation transaction, and CommercialExposureRules already accepts cancellation zero headers and omits their winning contribution. Reuse these paths under the existing common transaction fence rather than introducing mutable expiry updates. The new work is source/decision/closure validation and the cancellation writer connection.

A release must use the base exposure's exact binder/book and term, not a caller-supplied or newly guessed book. Consider the case where the wider book is already above a limit: releasing this policy reduces exposure and should not be treated as requesting additional capacity. The cancellation decision should retain exact source, actor authority and dated release provenance independently of a new-business capacity-approval outcome. Add an independent boundary case so a future refactor cannot make an already-overdrawn book prevent an otherwise authorised cancellation.

Published commercial definition verified in contracts/examples/commercial-underwriting-demo.json: authority commercial-demo-senior-1; binder commercial-demo-binder-1; rating commercial-demo-rate-1.
