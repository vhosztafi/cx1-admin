# 08-10 implementation decisions

These refinements apply the approved commercial policy read plan under delegated autonomy.

- `CommercialExposureReadModel.ReadAsync(actor, subjectKind, subjectId, effectiveAt?, knownAt?, token)` backs all three commercial exposure GETs. A transaction-owned shared `CoverMGA.CommercialExposure` lock precedes retained scope locks, matching the exclusive writer order. The observation therefore cannot mix a newly committed header, incomplete children and earlier limit publication.
- The endpoint accepts either both ISO offset timestamps or neither. Unknown, repeated, paging, district and agency filters are rejected. Knowledge cannot exceed observation time. Every response is `no-store`. Bound quotes reuse the policy observation and cannot add a second proposal contribution.
- Current internal policy-read authority or current agency-sharing-read scope authorizes the subject. Broker cookies are supported through the existing agency identity path. The current agency, actor, quote, client and relationship are checked before exposure is returned; foreign subjects have a uniform404. A new endpoint authorization policy does not broaden existing internal policy APIs.
- Policy observations select the exact immutable version at E/K, retain its source ID/hash, and compute whole-book totals at E. Scheduled, expired and cancelled slices contribute zero outside active cover. An unbound quote instead assesses its entire proposed term against knowledge at K. It never reserves capacity. Only the selected subject's districts are projected; aggregation never filters the underlying book by agency or discovery-list filters.
- Closed agency DTOs contain own contribution and outcome only. Internal DTOs additionally contain book ID, total, count and coherent limit/hash/headroom pins. Missing or ambiguous limits remain explicitly unavailable. A 1,000-row presentation bound does not truncate assessment before calculating its outcome. Monetary strings allow18 aggregate digits and the UI formats them without floating-point conversion.
- Draft routing performs actual parent and draft scope checks and identifies the retained revision. Until08-11/12 supplies a commercial change projection, it returns `commercial-exposure-draft-projection-unavailable`, with no invented base-policy preview. The negative/owned-draft HTTP cases exercise this real route. Draft capacity projection remains a mandatory follow-through in08-12.
- The policy component uses nine commercial tabs: Overview, Risk details, Cover, Property schedule, Liability & employees, Business interruption, Transactions, Documents and History. It renders selected issued facts, including two-location property amounts, exact wage categories, BI basis, selected sections, financial journal and document requests. The shared history component now accepts the explicit commercial snapshot union; Motor Trade behavior is retained.
- Demo MEL is the largest location's buildings + contents + stock total, calculated in integer pennies. It is labelled separately from any supplied loss estimate. Employee headcounts, wording authors and other uncaptured facts are explicitly unavailable; sampled prototype values are never fabricated. Current risk does not substitute for retained history. If a specifically opened version differs from the E/K exposure winner, the panel clearly identifies that mismatch.
- Fifty source controls are invalid global-tab Motor Trade fallbacks; the commercial tab set removes them. Agency, location and employee controls navigate actual owned records or tabs. Adjustment/renewal/cancellation stay with08-11..14; document delivery/incidents with Phase9; product configuration with Phase11. Their pending states are explicit. Commercial cancellation label/exposure rules are tested here; an issued commercial cancellation browser journey requires08-14's writer and remains that plan's gate.
- No new migration is needed. SQL/browser checks use owned test databases and dynamic API ports excluding5000. The running demo, its keys and the sales funnel are preserved.
