# 08-09 implementation decisions

These refine the approved atomic Commercial Combined first-issue plan under delegated autonomy.

- `CommercialExposureLock.Resource` is the fixed `CoverMGA.CommercialExposure` transaction-owned exclusive application lock. The command-key lock precedes it; the commercial fence precedes all retained quote/policy/draft scope locks. A read-committed product hint selects the fence without authorizing or disclosing the quote. Initialization takes the fence before its foundation lock. Projection, limit publication and decision SQL triggers require the fence.
- Current identity, parent scope and multidimensional authority are checked before idempotency receipt lookup. Replay does not reassess capacity. A new effect assesses the whole remaining cover period, including known future policy and limit boundaries. Lock timeout/negative acquisition results produce bounded `commercial-exposure-busy`; the browser keeps the original command key for retry.
- Book publication, binder authority and actor authority are independent ceilings. The selected current grant must cover every dimension and active condition. It is also the authority pinned in the issued snapshot. A larger published book limit cannot broaden an existing binder or staff grant. The immutable decision retains both authority amounts and grant/version identities alongside book limit pins and every assessed interval.
- `CommercialExposureIssueDecision` is an append-only one-to-one child of the first-issue exposure header. Header, complete locations, decision, policy/term/version, balanced financial posting, selected document requests, outbox, bound quote and exact command receipt share one transaction. SQL validates the decision source/hash/authority, complete own districts, contiguous full-term intervals, pinned published limits and nonnegative headroom.
- New Commercial Combined policy references use `PL-CC-` plus ten digits; Motor Trade retains `PL-MT-`. The additive prefix column defaults to the historical Motor Trade prefix, preserving pre-migration references. Prefix changes are prohibited. Downgrade refuses retained commercial issues and restores the computed reference before dropping its prefix dependency.
- The closed `issued-commercial-1` snapshot retains commercial capture facts and adds actual term, premium, provenance and selected sections. Property sections target one retained location; glass states the captured basis without inventing a monetary limit. Commercial schedule/statement are always requested; the existing certificate kind uses a commercial employers' liability template only when EL is selected. Motor Trade retains all three document kinds.
- The existing issue POST accepts the same closed command. Commercial receipts and policy readbacks additionally contain `commercialExposureDecisionId`. Capacity conflicts expose bounded aggregate intervals and pinned limits to the currently authorized internal issuer, without foreign policy identities. Advisory exposure GETs and the complete commercial policy/history interface remain plan08-10.
- The shared issue confirmation carries local product/EL expectations solely for receipt validation; they are not submitted as write authority. The initial commercial policy component renders the persisted source, postings and pending document requests, with a source-quote link. Unsupported commercial servicing stays unavailable until its owning plans.
- Tests use owned SQL databases, dynamic API ports explicitly excluding5000 and an isolated web build. The running demo, persistent keys and sales funnel remain preserved. Document generation/provider delivery are separate later work; first issue creates durable requests.
