- Extend the existing closed servicing change union with prefixed commercial kinds: location, property, business, BI, liability, wage, loss, cover, insured and declarations. The immutable base product dispatches validation; client input cannot select another product. Motor Trade retains its kinds and rejects commercial kinds.
- Location/wage/loss changes use stable row IDs; singleton business/BI/liability/cover/declarations target the policy ID, insured targets the retained client ID. Payloads cannot change client/relationship ownership or inject issued premium/sections. Explicit replacement clears omitted declarations while retaining envelope-owned identity. Property-only replacement clears only property amounts, preserving address/protections.
- Reuse the proposal canonicalizer, ETags, current-scope-before-replay and lease command boundary. The new pure projection reuses commercial capture shape/identity/readiness validation without manufacturing quote configuration pins. Shared effective dates and optional commercial-cover overrides produce cumulative immutable proposed slices. Save never publishes exposure, issues cover or creates an accounting effect.
- Reject foreign, duplicate, deleted/reused subjects, cross-product changes and identity relocation. London gap/fold, term bounds, latest issued effective date and current senior backdate authority remain independent readiness gates.
- Read editor pins the commercial question/reference catalogue. Current commercial base checks prevent saving over a superseding issued source. Only commercial adjustment creation is enabled here; renewal/cancellation have their own prerequisites in08-13/14. Commercial rating is explicitly unavailable until08-12 replaces that temporary gate with its own pipeline.
- Reuse the existing commercial capture field components for typed adjustment editors. Keep their local edit/discard dialogs and strict money/choice handling, but map edits into the servicing change envelope against the exact retained base. A separate commercial workspace keeps Motor Trade-only proof/rating/driver UI out of the flow while reusing the same HTTP command helpers.
- The commercial policy action opens an actual persisted adjustment draft. The commercial exposure draft read remains explicitly unavailable until08-12 assesses proposed slices against the full issued timeline and book. This is a required downstream integration, not an issued-base fallback.
